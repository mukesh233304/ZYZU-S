Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our home tutoring allotments, study space bookings, verification standards, and district governance.

Students & Parents

How does ZYZU'S ensure the tutor sent to my home is trustworthy?
Every tutor on ZYZU'S undergoes rigorous 4-step district verification: government ID (Aadhaar / Passport) authentication, university graduation degree verification, physical address verification, and approval by your official District Administrator.
Can I request a change if the tutor does not match my learning pace?
Yes! If you are not completely satisfied after the first introductory session, your District Administrator will reassign another qualified educator from your district at no extra charge, or issue a refund of unused tuition fees.
How do I book a desk in a self-study library?
You can browse affiliated libraries in your district on the Find Library page, select your preferred shift (Morning, Evening, 24/7) and desk from the interactive matrix, and book instantly. Your digital pass will be generated immediately in your Student Portal.
How do I track my daily classes and tuition fees?
Your Student Dashboard features a real-time Attendance and Fees tracker. Whenever your teacher completes a class, they mark attendance (Present, Absent, Late, Leave) and topic notes. You can view payment receipts and upcoming due dates directly.

Home Tutors

How are students allotted to me as a teacher?
On ZYZU'S, student requests are received and reviewed by your District Administrator. The District Admin matches students to you based on your verified subject expertise, location, and schedule availability. You only see students officially allotted to you on your Teacher Portal.
When and how do I receive my monthly earnings?
Tuition fees are secured in escrow when the student enrolls. As you mark attendance for completed sessions on your Teacher Portal, earnings are calculated automatically and disbursed directly to your registered bank account via RTGS/UPI at the start of each month.
What documents do I need to submit to get approved?
You must upload: (1) A valid government photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID), (2) Marksheets or degree certificate of your highest qualification, and (3) Bank account details. Your District Admin reviews these before approving your account.

Library Owners

Can I add walk-in students who do not have a ZYZU'S app account?
Yes, absolutely! The Library Owner Console includes a dedicated Manual Student Ledger. You can enter offline walk-in students with their name, phone, assigned desk, shift, and monthly fee without requiring them to download or register on the app.
How does the interactive desk matrix work?
Your dashboard provides a visual seat grid representing your library layout. Desks change color in real-time based on status (Green for Vacant, Red for Occupied, Blue for App Reserved) across different shifts (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night).
How are monthly fee dues tracked?
The system monitors renewal dates for both app bookings and manually added students. You get instant alerts for pending dues, overdue accounts, and can generate printable fee receipts with one click.

District Governance

What is the role of a District Administrator?
The District Admin is the dedicated operational overseer of their specific district. They verify local teacher credentials, inspect study libraries, process student tutor inquiries, pair teachers with learners, and resolve any local disputes.
Why is there strictly ONE active District Admin per district?
This invariant eliminates administrative conflicts, ensures clear accountability for safety and quality within that geographic jurisdiction, and guarantees consistent communication with local parents, schools, and library proprietors.
