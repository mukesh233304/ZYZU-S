TRANSPARENT SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

How ZYZU'S Operates

Explore the end-to-end operational lifecycle for students, educators, study spaces, and district governance.

1

Discover & Submit Tutor Request

Browse verified teachers in your district or submit an open learning inquiry stating your subject, grade, locality, days, and budget. The request is securely routed to your District Administrator.

2

District Admin Official Allotment

Your District Admin analyzes the learning requirements, matches a background-verified educator in your locality, and makes the formal teacher assignment.

3

1-on-1 Classes with Attendance Tracking

Tuition begins at your home. The teacher records digital attendance for each session. You can view attendance stats (Present, Absent, Late, Leave) and topic notes in your Student Portal.

4

Book Self-Study Library Desks

Whenever you need distraction-free quiet study, choose an affiliated library, select your preferred shift (Morning, Evening, 24/7) and desk number, and generate your instant digital desk pass.

1

Create Profile & Submit Credentials

Register on the ZYZU'S Teacher Portal. Specify your academic qualifications, subjects, classes, preferred service radius, and expected hourly rates.

2

District Verification & Compliance

Your District Administrator inspects your government ID and degree certificates. Once verified, your status changes to Approved with an official badge.

3

Receive Allotted Students

You only receive student assignments officially paired by your District Admin. You do not have to filter through unsolicited inquiries or compete in chaotic bidding wars.

4

Log Attendance & Receive Guaranteed Payouts

Log student attendance after every class. Payouts are automatically aggregated and disbursed directly into your bank account every month.

1

Register Study Space & Amenities

Enter your library name, address, total desk capacity, shift schedules, and amenities (Air Conditioning, High-Speed Wi-Fi, Generator Power Backup, RO Water).

2

District Verification Audit

The District Admin validates your facility safety, amenities, and location before activating your listing on the student discovery marketplace.

3

Manage Both App & Walk-In Students

Receive digital seat reservations from ZYZU'S students and use the Manual Student Ledger to record walk-in students without requiring an app account.

4

Visual Desk Matrix & Automated Dues

Track seat occupancy across all shifts on the interactive floor plan. The platform automatically flags pending monthly dues and generates receipts.

1

Sole District Operational Authority

Strict governance ensures that each district has exactly one active District Administrator assigned by the Super Admin, preventing conflicting jurisdictions.

2

Vetting Teachers & Facilities

The District Admin examines all teacher applications, reviews uploaded documents, and audits libraries within their specific district boundaries.

3

Tutor Matching & Assignment Engine

Student tutor requests arrive directly at the District Admin console. The Admin evaluates learner needs, searches suitable local educators, and executes the formal pairing.

4

Quality Monitoring & Grievance Resolution

Monitors tuition attendance compliance, tracks fee escrows, resolves parent or teacher queries, and issues disciplinary suspensions when necessary.
